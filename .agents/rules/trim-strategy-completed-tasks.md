# Strict Rule: Trim Completed Tasks from Strategy Files

Upon completion of any daily task or milestone:
1. Immediately remove/cut the completed task section from `day_by_day_strategy.md`.
2. Update the top summary block header (e.g. `> ✅ **Days 1–X Completed**: ...`) to include the newly completed work.
3. Ensure `day_by_day_strategy.md` ONLY lists upcoming, uncompleted tasks.

## Rationale
Prevents strategy roadmap documents from growing bloated and confusing. The document stays concise and focused exclusively on what's next.
